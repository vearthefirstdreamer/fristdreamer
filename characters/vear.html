Vear (formerly Kor)

Korranthisian from a high-gravity world; bearer of the Authority’s archive; symbol of resistance.

  • Species: Korranthisian
  • Status: Fugitive • First Dreamer
  • Destiny: Reluctant Time Traveler
Vear - Ancient Edsonian Era

Appearance

  • Tall, muscular; angular features; disciplined presence.
  • Tan skin; short tactical brown hair.
  • Eyes once deep blue; now concealed by the visor (Ember Crown).

Abilities & Enhancements

  • Hyper-Intelligence: Accidental upload of the Authority’s archive unlocked exceptional cognition.
  • High-G Physiology: Near-superhuman strength and speed on Edson.
  • Sensory Acuity: Tracks by heartbeat, air shift, and scent.
  • Strategic Genius: Intuitive systems thinking—biological, mechanical, political.
  • Untraceable: Silos Vorn’s modifications mask his biosignature.

Personality

  • Quiet, observant; acts with intent.
  • Fiercely loyal—particularly to those who are closest to him.
  • Burdened yet resolute; compassionate but guarded.

Philosophy & Motivation

  • Identity: Neither specimen nor prey—becoming something new.
  • Purpose: To understand who and what he is—biologically and cosmically.
  • Legacy: Chooses the name Vear to become the legend history remembers.
  • Freedom: Runs toward a future beyond surveillance and fear.

Loss of vision

  • Battle: Vear lost his sight during a violent battle with Agent Thalos aboard a Beta-Class Time Ship, in orbit over Edson during the Middle Edsonian Era (~-1000 AE).
  • Escape: Gravely injured but alive, Vear escaped aboard the Aetherion, flinging himself deeper into the past—10,000 years earlier.
  • Adapt: Now stranded in ancient Edson and learning to live without physical vision, he became a symbol to the people he protected.

The Visor

  • The Gift: The villagers, led by Thomel, gift him a visor as a tribute and sign of trust. The visor contains a Green Crystal.
  • Crystal of Clarity: The Crystal unlocks his ability to see with his mind, granting him advanced perception beyond the physical senses
  • Affinity: It also allows him perceive the emotions and intentions of others with uncanny accuracy.

How Vear Compensates For His Blindness With Out his Visor

Biological Adaptation

Hearing, smell, and touch sharpen to map space—footstep vibrations, air currents, and scent become data.

Shadow Perception

Renders nearby forms as faint, spatially accurate silhouettes—enough to move and fight with confidence.

Perceiving the Non-Physical

Detects presences like the Aetherion’s holographic AI (Aethra), giving “shape” to what others only see as light.

Integrated Navigation

Combines shadow-sense with memory, sound, and touch to build precise mental maps after a single pass.

Instinct & Reflexes

In danger, reacts to breath, motion, and pressure—timing that borders on prescient.

Tactile & Electronic Reading

Reads printed ink by texture; ship systems translate data via audio/haptics or projections perceptible via shadow-sense.

In the Narrative

  • First Contact: The Authority arrives from the future in a massive ship, seen by Kor’s people as gods. In reality, they are descendants of Earth, long dead for over 100,000 years.
  • Salvation Program: His people are labeled primitive and slated for “rescue” from a meteor impact predicted to occur in 100 years.
  • Resistance: Kor senses danger in their “mercy” and intends to resist but is taken before he can fight back.
  • Data Flood Incident: In an Authority lab on the ship, a system malfunction—or unseen manipulation—uploads the entire Authority knowledge archive into Kor’s brain. All others subjected to the process die; only Kor survives.
  • Adaptation: Kor pretends to be primitive to hide his intellect, studying in silence.
  • Bond Formation: Kor builds a deep friendship with Lyra, who slowly discovers his intelligence.
  • Secret Projects: He rebuilds her old school tablet, uncovering a hidden Archive containing information absent from the Authority’s upload and discovers the location of the the morphogenic re-sequencer
  • Transformation: Uses the morphogenic re-sequencer to become human, adopts the name Vear, taken from an ancient Edsonian legend that paradoxically originates with himself.
  • Flight and Infiltration: Over years, evades the Authority while creating a false digital identity (“Kelan Vear”) to infiltrate their systems.
  • Time Travel: Events eventually push Kor into Edson’s past—first 1,000 years, then 10,000 years before Lyra’s time.
  • Rise to Legend: Protects villages, inspires rebellion, and becomes a figure of myth.
