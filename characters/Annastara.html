Annastara

Daughter of Vear and Alara. A quiet heir to a legend, marked by perception, strength, and intelligence beyond her years. Those close to her sometimes call her Ann..

Profile

Annastara is six years old at her introduction in Book II. She bears dark hair and a light complexion, with eyes unmistakably her mother’s. Subtle but clear signs suggest she has inherited more than lineage—there are echoes of Vear’s legacy in her strength and intelligence.

Abilities

Physical Aptitude: Displays strength well beyond age norms, reflecting traits linked to Vear’s high-gravity heritage.

Cognitive Insight: Learns quickly, discerns patterns, and adapts with unusual ease—early markers of exceptional intelligence.

Narrative Role

Annastara is introduced in Book II as a living bridge between Vear’s legend and the uncertain future of Edson. Her abilities remain understated at first reveal, preserving mystery while hinting at her importance in the unfolding story.

Codex (In-Universe)

“ANN-ST-2: Provisional. Minor—offspring of Subject VE-AR and AL-RA. Monitor for non-baseline growth in strength and cognition. Public disclosure deferred until post-Authority review.”

Annastara — The Stoneborn Child

Small. Human. And unlike anything the MoRoc have ever known.

Profile

Annastara appears small and human, a fragile child in a world shaped by stone and endurance. Yet the wings rising from her back tell another story.

Pale and translucent, they remain caught somewhere between living tissue and mineral structure, their delicate frames carrying faint pulses of blue light. They emerge naturally from her upper back, where subtle traces of mineralization fade gradually into human skin—the last visible evidence of a transformation she does not yet fully understand.

Even the simple tunic prepared for her reflects how different she has become. Its back is left open around the wings, allowing them to move freely while preserving the ordinary comfort of clothing she can remove, clean, and wear without restraint.

She is neither entirely what she was nor entirely what she is becoming.

She is Annastara—the Stoneborn Child.

Age9, nearly 10
HairDark

The Stoneborn Child Narrative Role

Introduced in Book III, Annastara is imprisoned within the Mother Stone on Terafna by Vel Tharuun, only to emerge a thousand years later, reborn as the Stoneborn Child. Her rebirth marks the beginning of a struggle for survival in a world that fears what she is and seeks to keep her isolated. Forced to confront suspicion, rejection, and loneliness, Annastara must find the strength to survive in a world where simply existing makes her an outsider.
