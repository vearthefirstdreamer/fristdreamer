The Rulie

While others sought to master the universe, the Rulie chose to understand it — and in doing so, became part of it.

  • Species: Rulie (not human)
  • Status: unkown
  • Era / Locale:The Forgotten Era (-20,000 to -11,000 AE) / unknown
Rulie with Staff of Resonance

Appearance

  • Height: Between 5'10" and 6'8", shorter than legends claim. Many stoop slightly, making them seem even smaller, yet they carry an undeniable presence.
  • Build: Slender but not frail, with long limbs and elongated torsos. Their movements are slow, purposeful—like underwater dancers or monks at prayer.
  • Posture: Characteristically hunched forward, not from age or pain, but as if they are always listening—to the ground, to time, or to the universe itself.
  • Skin: Smooth, pale violet, or mist-silver, sometimes dappled with faint natural patterns like mineral veins or ripples.
  • Head:
    • Shape resembles a giant sloth, elongated and solemn, with a wide, downward-curving brow.
    • No nose—only slits and smooth contours.
    • Eyes: Large, ovular, fully black or glowing indigo; unblinking and reflective.
    • No ears—just subtle ridges along the sides of the skull that sense resonant frequencies.

Clothing & Tools:

  • Robes: Silken layers made from woven crystal threads and organic fibers. Always flowing, with quiet, rippling patterns—some shift subtly in the presence of certain emotions or harmonic fields.
  • Staff of Resonance:
    • Usually as tall as the Rulie themself. Topped with a crescent moon design.
    • A blue crystal floats inside the crescent, humming faintly in proximity to sound, memory, or power.
    • Used for sensing, healing, balancing energies—not combat.
    • Each staff is soul-bonded and cannot be used by another.

Biological & Cognitive Profile

  • Neurostructure: Their brains are multi-phase quantum-organic matrices, meaning they think across multiple dimensions of probability. Their "thoughts" are harmonics rather than linear language.
  • Time Perception: Experience time nonlinearly — not full time travelers, but capable of multi-temporal awareness (e.g., sensing echoes of future consequences).
  • Memory: Their memory is distributed, partially embedded in their physical form, partially in harmonic fields linked to nearby crystals and planetary systems.
  • Communication: Transmit meaning via harmonic resonance, emotion-infused frequencies that bypass spoken language. They can “think” in equations, tones, and geometric constructs simultaneously.

Relationship to the Crystals

  • The Rulie discovered the Clear Crystal, not created it — they believe it is a natural artifact from the universe’s first harmonic resonance.
  • All colored crystals (red, dreeb, blue, grey, black) are refractions of the original Clear Crystal — each representing a distorted but powerful principle.
  • They forged the Star Sword using Blue Crystal — believing it could channel Will without corruption.
  • The Black Crystal is to them a paradox — raw potential without boundary — and therefore dangerous beyond all measure.

Relationship to Elias’s Rac

  • They are not enemies, but ideological opposites:
    • Elias’s kind seeks mastery of energy and potential, even if it leads to manipulation.
    • The Rulie seek resonance and surrender to pattern, even if it leads to obscurity.
    • It is said they once met in an age before memory, and neither won, nor lost, but came to an accord:
      • “Where one shapes, the other tunes. Where one questions, the other listens.”

Narrative Possibilities (Book 2)

  • Vear could encounter a Rulie holographic echo or living elder, who senses his presence as a “discordant thread” — not a threat, but a pivotal moment.
  • The Star Sword might only reach its full potential when a Rulie harmonic key unlocks the last resonance locked inside the Blue Crystal.
  • The Rulie and Elias may both appear to Leia or Vear with conflicting truths about the Black Crystal — leading to a profound moral choice.
