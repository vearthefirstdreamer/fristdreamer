Agent Thalos

Relentless Authority enforcer, master tactician, and the most dangerous adversary Vear has ever faced.

  • Species: Human (Authority Homeworld)
  • Status: Elite Operative • Authority Security Bureau • Head of the Serpent Force—an elite black-ops unit
  • Era / Locale: Authority Era & Ancient Edson
Agent Thalos wearing the uniform of the elite Serpent Force.

Appearance

  • Tall, imposing; combat-trained physique.
  • Sometime's wears a sealed black combat suit with a featureless helmet and other times wears the uniform of the elite Serpent Force.
  • Helmet equipped with advanced sensors and encrypted comms.

Abilities & Equipment

  • Strategic Operative: Trained in both battlefield command and covert operations.
  • Cybernetic Augmentations: Enhanced strength, reflexes, and sensory integration.
  • Advanced Tracking: Can detect bio-signatures through heavy interference.
  • Combat Mastery: Skilled in ranged and close-quarters combat.

Personality

  • Cold, calculating, and uncompromising.
  • Driven by an unshakable belief in Authority supremacy.
  • Sees pursuit of Vear as both a mission and a personal challenge.

Motivations

  • Order: Believes chaos must be crushed at any cost.
  • Control: Views the Authority as the rightful ruler of all civilized worlds.
  • Personal Pride: Refuses to be outmaneuvered by a fugitive like Vear.

Role in the Narrative

Thalos is more than a hunter—he is the embodiment of the Authority’s reach and relentlessness. His pursuit of Vear spans worlds and centuries, his tactics adapting to every setback. Where others underestimate Vear, Thalos studies him, anticipating his moves. Their encounters are battles of mind and body, each pushing the other to their limits.

In the Narrative

  • First appears as the lead Authority agent overseeing Kor’s capture.
  • Follows Vear into the past, determined to end the threat he poses.
  • Serves as the constant shadow haunting Vear’s journey.
