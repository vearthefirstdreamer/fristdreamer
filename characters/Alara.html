Alara of Edson (Alara Thalen)

Alara, daughter of Marek—heir to a crown, yet sworn to the sword.

  • Species:Human (Planet Edson)
  • Status:heir to a crown, yet sworn to the sword
  • Era / Locale:Middle Edsonian Era (-1000 AE AE)/Arden
Alara of Edson

Appearance

  • Age: 19
  • Tall and lithe, with the posture of a born warrior
  • Wears flexible dark steel armor etched with the sigil of Arden (a rising falcon)
  • Long dark auburn braid often tied into a loop when in armor
  • Piercing grey-green eyes, always watching and calculating
  • Carries a slender longblade and a recurved throwing knife of her own design

Personality Traits

  • Passionate but disciplined; proud but not arrogant
  • Fiercely loyal to her kingdom—but willing to break with tradition when justice demands it
  • Deeply empathetic, but hardened by battlefield loss
  • Has no patience for political games or weak-willed councilors

Legacy

  • Later remembered as Alara the Flame, the one who rode beside the First Dreamer.
  • Her battlefield tactics become part of Ardan military lore.
  • After Vear vanishes, her fate becomes the subject of legend—some say she led one final campaign, Some claim that she ascended to the throne of Arden, others say she disappeared into the mist with him.
  • Her name is carved into the ruins of a temple Vear once helped her defend.

Profile:

At only 19 years old, Alara has already earned the rank of Commander of the Ardan Guard, a position she fought for in both the court and the field. As the only child of King Marek, she was expected to become a diplomat or consort—but instead, she became a soldier.

Tactically sharp and physically formidable, she leads from the front, earning the devotion of her soldiers and the wary respect of older generals. When Vear arrives, mysterious and powerful, she doesn’t fear him—she challenges him. And soon, she chooses to stand at his side.

Authority Recovered Intelligence Brief — Subject: “Alara Thalen”:

Source Integrity: 43% (fragmentary)

Primary Origin: Edson — Middle Era (~1,000 years before Authority Era 0)

Cross-Reference Tags: “Daughter of Marek,” “Arden Royal Line,” “Witness to the Skyborn” "Possible Accession to crown"

Surviving inscriptions and partial oral accounts identify the subject as Alara, Daughter of Marek, a highborn figure associated with the capital of Arden. Translation inconsistencies have led to multiple designations; “Thalen” appears in only one recovered registry, possibly a mistranslation, false attribution, or name adopted through contact with early offworld travelers.

The “Skyborn” designation appears in two unrelated sources from the same period, context unclear. Some interpretations suggest it may refer to a local myth or unidentified celestial visitor, though no corroborating records have been confirmed.

Historical certainty is moderate. Many planetary records from this era were destroyed during the early Collapse, but select fragments survived in Authority pre-expansion archives and scavenged data cores.

Assessment: Subject may hold relevance to pre-Collapse political movements and unverified accounts of anomalous phenomena. Further study of Arden ruins and related timelines recommended.

In the Narrative

  • First to recognize Vear’s discipline and depth
  • Helps him integrate into Ardan society and eventually the military structure
  • Leads joint campaigns with Vear against rising threats in Edson’s tribal past
  • Becomes his closest confidant and strategic partner during his presence
  • Their relationship grows intimate—defined by trust, mutual respect, and unspoken emotion
  • United in marriage while maintaining secrecy

In time, Alara would become the mother of Annastara, a child whose future role remains unwritten but whose presence would echo both parents' legacies.
