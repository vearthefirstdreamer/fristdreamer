Nick Yost – Author of The Kor Cycle

Nick Yost is a speculative fiction author and retired software engineer, best known for The Kor Cycle series. His work blends science fiction, philosophical themes, and character-driven storytelling.

The Kor Cycle Series

  • Vear – The Odyssey of the Dreamer (Book 1)
  • Vear – The Price of Possibilities (Book 2)

The Kor Cycle follows the journey of Kor, a young being whose evolution into Vear reshapes the fate of civilizations across time and space. The series explores identity, power, and the cost of knowledge.

About the Author

Nick Yost writes science fiction that explores the boundaries between technology, consciousness, and human experience. He is the creator of The Kor Cycle and continues to develop the series.
