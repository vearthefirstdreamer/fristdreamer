Vel Tharuun’s Perspective

The void‑bound mind that seeks the Black Crystal finds a signal in Annastara — a path to rebirth through living stone and will‑colored crystal.

Imprisonment Nature of the Black Crystal Living Stone Sensing Annastara Proof & Intent Narrative Implications

Imprisonment in the Void

Silenced by the Council — but not ended.

Vel Tharuun was bound to the void by Elias and the Council of Higher Minds for his obsession with the Black Crystal.

Nature of the Black Crystal

Not an object alone — a fusion.

  • More than matter: a union of crystal and mind.
  • Clear crystal becomes black when shaped by a will strong enough to bend time.

The Living Stone of Terafna

Within it lie countless dormant clear crystals, waiting to resonate with a mind powerful enough to shape them.

Sensing Annastara

When Annastara fuses with the Living Stone, Vel Tharuun senses the event instantly — her will, carrying Vear’s bloodline, awakens the stone and turns its clear veins blue.

Proof & Intent

To Vel Tharuun, the blue is proof: Annastara is the seed of a new Black Crystal — a vessel in which crystal and mind may again become one.

Narrative Implications

  • MoRoc & Humans: They see only strangeness and fear.
  • Vel Tharuun: He sees a chance to recreate the Black Crystal within her and free himself from the void.
  • Annastara’s Flight: No mere power, but a signpost — the vessel he has awaited.
  • Whispered Influence: His shadow slips into her dreams, shaping her toward a living Black Crystal.
“To the MoRoc: a sister set apart. To humans: a monster unnatural. To me: destiny — the key.” — Vel Tharuun
