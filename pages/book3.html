Outline – Book 3: The Stoneborn Child

Annastara returns from the Abyss reborn within living stone on Terafna. Between human fear, MoRoc ritual, and Vel Tharuun’s whisper, her childhood becomes a crucible. Across the century-spanning weave, Vear and Lyra are drawn forward to face what she has become—and what the Devouring Mind still intends.

Prologue

  • Elias narrates: the Chamber of Despair → Annastara’s abduction → her rebirth at age 7 within the Mother Stone on Terafna.
  • The MoRoc welcome her as “sister,” unaware of what she truly is.
  • Elias hints that Vel Tharuun’s shadow lingers in her return.

Act I – The Stone Child (Age 7)

Theme: Awakening
  • The Birth of Stone and Flesh: Annastara emerges from the Mother Stone at seven years old. The MoRoc embrace her; humans whisper of a curse.
  • Among MoRoc: Some hail her as chosen; others fear her. She learns discipline, ritual, and fragments of ancient wisdom. Prophecy stirs: “The child of stone and flesh will choose for all.”
  • The Lost Child: Unaware of what she truly is Annastara comes across a human village who welcome her in as a lost child.
↑ Back to top

Act II – The Years of Trial (Ages 7–9)

Theme: Rejection and Growth
  • Among Humans: She experiences life in a human community; earns acceptance and starts to feel comfortable
  • The First Transformation: Taunted by another child, she instinctively turns to stone. Humans witness the moment Annastara’s stone wings appear — and the village brands her a monster. She escapes, heartbroken.
  • The Lonely Path: Neither MoRoc nor humans fully accept her; she wanders between two worlds.
  • The MoRaw Song: Annastara meets Teano (Water Sprite) of the MoRaw by a narrow stream — a kinship revealed in water’s voice.
    • The MoRem Song: A MoRem shapeshifter reveals himself and challenges Annastara’s place among the Mo‑born.
  • Whispers of the Void: Vel Tharuun’s voice seeps into her dreams, promising belonging and power.
↑ Back to top

Act III – The Shadow Grows (Age 9)

Theme: Temptation and Power
  • The Gathering of Enemies: Authority remnants and other seekers reach Terafna, drawn by tales of the “stone child.” Humans and MoRoc divide over her fate.
  • Stone and Flesh in Balance: Annastara learns control—shifting at will—but each use tethers her more tightly to the Mother Stone.
  • The Temptation of Vel Tharuun: “You are mine. You were born of the stone, but your soul is shadow.” She nearly yields, then resists—for now.
↑ Back to top

Act IV – The Circle of Time (Age 10)

Theme: Destiny and Inheritance
  • The Rift of Years: Through Elias’s hand, Authority relics, or the Mother Stone itself, Vear and Lyra are drawn forward. They arrive on Terafna a thousand years after the abduction; Annastara is ten.
  • The Reunion: Vear—once transformed at ten—meets his daughter at ten. Lyra embraces her, though the child is now more MoRoc than human. Annastara remembers them, but feels bound to another world.
  • The Choice of the Stoneborn: She stands between humans, MoRoc, and Vel Tharuun’s looming shadow. Her decision will shape Terafna—and perhaps the universe.

Epilogue

  • Elias reflects: “Ten is the age of becoming. For Vear, it was the moment he passed as human. For Annastara, it is the moment she becomes something more.”
  • Ominous close: Vel Tharuun’s shadow has not been banished—only delayed.
