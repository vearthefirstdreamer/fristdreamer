The Blue Crystal Principle

Blue crystals govern gravitic balance — lightening or intensifying mass. From the anchored Starswords to Annastara’s living stone, this principle bends destiny and gravity alike.

Principle Starswords How Annastara’s Flight Works Risk Lore Tie‑In

Principle

Blue crystals master gravitic balance.

Effect
They can lighten or intensify mass, subtly shifting local gravity fields.
Application
In living hosts, blue‑crystal filaments can awaken during transformation to alter apparent weight.

Starswords

Anchored by blue crystal.

Myth
The Starswords were nearly immovable unless their wielder was chosen, the blue crystal within them “anchoring” their weight.
Reading
Selection wasn’t strength alone — it was resonance with the crystal’s gravitic will.

How Annastara’s Flight Works

When Annastara transforms, blue‑crystal filaments awaken throughout her stone form, especially along the wing struts and membranes.

Activation
Veins of blue light flare across her wings as crystal‑laced stone resonates with gravity itself.
Apparent Mass
To observers, “stone should not fly,” yet her adjusted weight is comparable to flesh and bone during flight.
Lift & Sustain
Her wings catch air like a bird’s; the gravity‑bending effect sustains glide and enables short powered bursts.

Risk

If her gravitic resonance falters mid‑flight, her body reverts to uncompensated stone mass.

Consequence: sudden loss of lift and catastrophic fall — a collapse “like dead stone.”

Lore Tie‑In

  • The MoRoc whisper she is “stone made sky‑light”, unlike their kind bound to earth.
  • Humans, witnessing her flight, whisper Skyborn in fear and reverence.
