Origin of Annastara’s Blue Crystal Veins

Dormant crystal in Living Stone. A will strong enough to color it. A child reborn who bends gravity with blue‑lit veins.

Living Stone Rebirth Mind & Will Ignition Implications Vel Tharuun

The Living Stone

MoRoc bodies contain dormant clear crystal strands.

These strands are inert until bonded to a will strong enough to shape them.

Rebirth in the Mother Stone

Annastara’s second genesis.

When she was reborn from the Mother Stone, the clear crystal veins fused into her flesh.

Mind & Will

Vear’s daughter bears a singular mind — informed by the Authority’s databanks, tempered by Edson’s struggle, sharpened by survival.

Ignition of the Blue

First Transformation
At her first true change, Annastara’s will awakened the fused clear crystal — turning it blue.
Result
Her stone form is crystal‑infused living stone, animated by will and gravity‑bending veins of blue.

Implications

  • The MoRoc whisper she is “not of their kind”; their stone lacks awakened crystal veins.
  • Humans see an impossibility: a gargoyle that flies while statues remain earthbound.
“The child is no accident. She is the Living Stone reborn — but tempered with the spark of a mind strong enough to color the crystal. Do you understand what this means, Vear? She is more than MoRoc… she is possibility itself.” — Elias

Vel Tharuun’s Shadow

A being who can awaken crystal by her very existence is the vessel Vel Tharuun would covet for the Black Crystal.

  • Logical: follows the crystal rule: clear → awakened by mind → blue.
  • Symbolic: only Vear’s bloodline could ignite such power.
