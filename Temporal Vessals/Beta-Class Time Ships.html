The Beta-Class (Space/Time Ship)

“Beta-Class Time Ships are colossal, city-sized temporal war vessels that rip open time itself, arriving not with precision but as planet-shaking invasions.”

Description

Beta-Class Time Ships are the Authority’s earliest large-scale temporal vessels—designed not for elegance or precision, but for brute-force incursions into the past. Each ship is the size of a floating city, built around a bulk-phase temporal core capable of tearing open stabilized corridors in time.

These ships are not surgical. They are invasions. When they arrive, planets tremble, the skies warp.

The Temporal Systems

  • Uses a Bulk-Phase Temporal Engine: opens corridors wide enough to move entire fleets
  • Consumes energy equivalent to vaporizing a moon just to stabilize a single jump
  • Corridor stability is poor—jumps often leave temporal radiation scars and echo signatures detectable for centuries
  • Known for time drift errors—sometimes arrive years off-target

Nickname: "Slave Ships"

  • So named because they were used for mass abductions from the past—entire villages, species, or populations pulled from pre-Authority eras
  • Prisoners were stored in cryo or stasis for analysis, experimentation, or repurposing
  • Corridor stability is poor—jumps often leave temporal radiation scars and echo signatures detectable for centuries
  • Survivors from these raids (like Kor) often remember them only as “the ships that tore open the sky”

Kor’s Capture

  • Kor was taken aboard a Beta-Class vessel deployed during a deep-time raid on Korrus, around -20,000 AE
  • The mission’s official goal: “Species Preservation and Genetic Archive”
  • The real motive: testing Authority control over pre-conscious lifeforms for military and civilian repurposing
  • Beta-Class ships were expected to be retired due to the rise of more subtle temporal tools—like the Aetherion and Black Veil (that never happened)

Visual Impression

  • Appears like a falling fortress when entering a planet’s atmosphere
  • Their arrival causes massive environmental disruption: tides shift, electromagnetic fields spike, plant and animal life collapses

Names for the Beta-class vessels:

Axiom

  • Meaning: An undeniable truth. The ship represents the Authority’s unquestionable dominance and belief in control through certainty.
  • Tone: Cold. Precise. Imposing
  • Nickname (used by crew or rebels): "The Truth Ship"

Gravemind

  • Meaning: Refers to memory, death, and overwhelming knowledge. A nod to its vast databanks and its role in erasing civilizations.
  • Tone: Haunting. Methodical. Silent.
  • Nickname: "The Collector

Dominion

  • Meaning: Total rule or sovereignty—especially over time and space.
  • Tone: Authoritarian. Towering. Absolute.
  • Nickname: "The Kingkiller"

Legacy:

  • The term “Beta-Class” is now synonymous with brutality and time violation
  • Survivors of their incursions, like Kor, retain deep psychological scars, even if they don't understand the full scope
